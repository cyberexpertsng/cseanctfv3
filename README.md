# cseanctfv3

The Cyber Security Experts Association of Nigeria, Nigeria's leading professional cybersecurity association dedicated to advancing cyber resilience and digital security awareness, held its annual Cyber Secure Nigeria 2026 Conference in Abuja on May 6th and 7th, 2026. The conference brought together cybersecurity professionals, policymakers, researchers, students, and industry leaders from across Africa to discuss emerging threats, digital resilience, governance, and the future of cybersecurity on the continent.

This year's edition featured multiple activities including keynote sessions, workshops, security villages, exhibitions, networking events, and a competitive Capture The Flag (CTF) competition focused on offensive and defensive security skills. The event was hosted at the Shehu Musa Yar’Adua Event Center in Abuja under the theme “Securing the Digital Future of Africa.”

Participants from different institutions and cybersecurity communities across Nigeria competed in various challenge categories including:
- Binary Exploitation
- Reverse Engineering
- Web Exploitation
- Digital Forensics
- Incident Response
- Network Security
- Cryptography
- Security Operations

The competition followed a jeopardy-style format where teams solved challenges to obtain flags and accumulate points on a live scoreboard. Team collaboration and communication were facilitated through Discord.

A total of 26 Teams successfully applied through the registration portal. 17 Teams made it to playing the CTF after thorough vetting as listed below:

After a thrilling competition, Team **airforce-ng** emerged as the overall winner with an impressive total of 8200 points. Team **Pwn_NG** and **Amara** secured the first runner-up and second runner-up positions with **8100** and **7950** points, respectively.
